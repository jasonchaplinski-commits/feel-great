What's in the box

The system is two products you mix with water. Times above are an example; shift the whole day to fit your schedule.

Unimate

Morning, and again at lunch or mid-afternoon

A yerba mate drink for your morning. Unicity says it supports focus and helps you feel full, and it won't break your fast.

Balance

Right before or with your heaviest meal

A fiber drink you take with your biggest meal of the day. Shake it hard and drink it right away, before it thickens.

Three simple steps

  1. AM

    Start with Unimate

    Mix it up first thing. You can take it again with lunch or as an afternoon pick-me-up.

  2. PM

    Balance with your big meal

    Drink it just before or with the heaviest meal of your day.

  3. 16h

    Fast overnight

    After dinner, wait about 16 hours before your next meal. Most of it happens while you sleep.

Tips that make it easier

  • Use a shaker bottle for Balance and drink it as soon as it's mixed.
  • Start with a shorter fast and work up to 16 hours if that feels better.
  • Drink plenty of water through the day, especially while fasting.
  • Set phone reminders for your Unimate and Balance times for the first couple of weeks.
